What Personal Information We Collect

We limit the personal information we collect to what is genuinely necessary for you to use Nuclear Tutorials. Depending on how you interact with the platform, we collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Account and Profile Information Provided by You

  • Name / Display Name: Your name or preferred display name (optional when registering an account, editable in your Learning Profile, and required when submitting a package payment enquiry).
  • Email Address: Used as your unique sign-in identifier and to deliver confirmed access codes and important account communications.
  • Password Credentials: When you create an account, your password is never stored in plain text. It is immediately converted into a salted cryptographic hash (using scrypt) before being stored in our database.
  • Learner Profile Preferences: What you are studying (University or High School), your selected university or school from the catalogue, and your selected semester or term (Semester 1 or Semester 2).
  • Linked Access Codes: Any existing redeemed access code that you choose to link to your account during registration or on the Access page.

2.2 Payment Enquiry and WhatsApp Communication Information

  • Payment Enquiry Details: When you request an access package on the Checkout page, we record your full name, email address, selected package tier (Basic, Standard, or Premium), the server-verified package price in Zambian Kwacha (ZMW), a unique payment reference (such as NT-ENQ-XXXXX), the status of the request (pending, confirmed, or rejected), the single-use access code issued upon confirmation, and confirmation email delivery logs.
  • Phone Number and WhatsApp Details: When you contact Nuclear Tutorials via WhatsApp or phone at +260 764 599 915 to complete a payment enquiry or ask an academic or support question, we receive your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of the messages or payment confirmation details you share with us.

2.3 Information Generated Through Your Use of the Platform

  • Access Grant Metadata: The package level unlocked on your account, the access code redeemed, the date and time of redemption, and the date your package access expires.
  • Learning Progress Records: Which video lessons you have opened or marked as watched/completed, your playback progress in seconds where recorded, and the timestamp of your last activity on each lesson.
  • Authentication Session Records: A randomly generated cryptographic session token, creation timestamp, and expiry timestamp that keep you signed in across pages.

2.4 Device, Browser, IP Address, Log and Technical Information

  • Connection and Network Information: Your IP address and reverse-proxy headers (X-Forwarded-For and X-Forwarded-Proto) received when your device connects to our server. On the payment enquiry endpoint, client IP addresses are held in server memory for a 10-minute window to rate-limit automated requests (up to 8 requests per 10 minutes per IP).
  • HTTP Request and Diagnostic Information: Standard HTTP request data processed in memory to serve pages and API responses (including requested URL path, HTTP method, Cookie, Origin, Range, and custom code header X-NT-Code) as well as server console error logs if a technical fault occurs.
  • Device-Local Preferences: Study preferences and active access metadata saved in your browser's localStorage under the key nt_student_state_v2 so your interface loads with your chosen institution and semester.

How We Apply the Principles of the Zambian Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021

Nuclear Tutorials designs its platform and student workflows around the core principles set out in the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021:

  • Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency: We explain in plain language what data we collect, why we collect it, and how payments and access codes work—with no hidden data harvesting or surprise charges.
  • Purpose Limitation: Personal information collected to provide your tutorial access and track your study progress is used only for those educational and operational purposes, and is never repurposed for unrelated commercial profiling.
  • Data Minimisation: We only ask for the minimum information needed. For example, your display name is optional at signup, and we do not collect national registration numbers, residential addresses, or bank card numbers on the website.
  • Accuracy: You can update your display name, learner type, institution, and semester at any time from your Learning Profile, or ask us to correct any inaccurate account or payment-enquiry record.
  • Storage Limitation: We keep personal data only for as long as needed to support your active account, honour your access package period, verify transactions, or satisfy legal obligations.
  • Integrity and Confidentiality: We protect personal information using salted password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, server-side access enforcement, and restricted operator controls.
  • Accountability: We take responsibility for handling student data carefully and provide direct contact channels (mandasteven23@gmail.com and +260 764 599 915) for any privacy question or request.

Account Authentication & Security

When you create a Nuclear Tutorials account, we require a password of between 10 and 256 characters. Your password is hashed on the server using Node.js cryptographic scrypt with a unique 16-byte random salt per account and compared using constant-time verification to help protect against timing attacks and account enumeration.

Once you sign in, our server issues a random 256-bit session token stored in an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie named nt_learner (marked Secure whenever accessed over HTTPS). Because this cookie is HttpOnly, client-side scripts cannot read your session token. When you log out, the session token is deleted from both our database and your browser cookie.

Nuclear Tutorials uses only strictly necessary first-party cookies and browser storage required for the platform to function:

Data Security Measures

We implement concrete technical safeguards in our codebase to help protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Student and operator passwords are hashed with Node.js scrypt and unique 16-byte random salts, and verified using constant-time comparison; plain-text passwords are never written to disk or returned by any API endpoint.
  • Protected Session Cookies: Sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies (with the Secure attribute enabled over HTTPS connections).
  • Server-Side Access Control: Lesson unlock status and package tiers are verified on the server for every request; protected video source URLs are withheld from unauthorised responses.
  • High-Entropy Access Codes: Access codes are generated using 96 bits of cryptographic randomness so unredeemed codes cannot be guessed.
  • Rate Limiting & Path Protection: Public payment enquiry routes are rate-limited in memory, and server source files, database files, and environment configurations are blocked from public static access.

We do not claim external security certifications, formal registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, or guaranteed immunity from security risks—no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You also help keep your account safe by choosing a strong password and keeping your access code confidential.

Your Data Protection Rights & How to Exercise Them

Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, you have clear rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of your account profile, payment enquiries, redeemed access codes, and saved learning progress.
  • Right to Correction (Rectification): You can update your display name, learner type, institution, and semester directly on your Learning Profile page at any time. You can also contact us to correct an email address, name, or payment enquiry record.
  • Right to Deletion (Erasure): You may request that we delete your student account and associated personal information where it is no longer needed for the purposes for which it was collected, where you withdraw consent, or where otherwise applicable under Zambian law (subject to any transaction records we are legally required or entitled to retain for payment verification).
  • Right to Object to or Restrict Processing: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data or request that we restrict processing in the circumstances provided by the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021.
  • Right to Data Portability: Where legally applicable, you may request that the personal information you provided to us be supplied to you in a structured, commonly used, and readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent to process personal information, you may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
