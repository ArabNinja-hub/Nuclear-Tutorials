FAQ

Questions students ask

How do I receive my access code?
Immediately after payment the platform issues a code such as NT-STANDARD-4821 on your receipt. Enter it on the Access page — or sign in straight away, since payment activates your package automatically in this demo.
What happens to locked lessons?
They stay visible in the library so you know what you're missing, but the video itself is never exposed. Upgrading unlocks them instantly — your progress is kept.
Can I upgrade mid-way?
Yes. Buy the higher package and your access level rises immediately; everything you had before stays unlocked.
Is this a real payment?
No. This is a client demonstration: checkout, mobile-money prompts and codes are fully simulated. No money moves and no real account is contacted.
